Explain • Foundation

What is a Logic Gate?

⚡

Building Block of Computers

A logic gate is a basic building block of digital circuits. It performs logical operations using binary inputs (0 or 1) to produce a single output signal.

📱

Transistors as Micro-Switches

Logic gates rely on transistors—tiny electrical switches that turn current ON and OFF.

Elaborate • 1-Minute Reflection

Impact of Logic Gates in Daily Life

"How do basic logic gates impact the electronic devices you use every day?"

Logic gates are essential because every single action on our smartphones, laptops, smart TVs, and computers relies on millions of microscopic transistors making ultra-fast binary decisions using these exact gate rules!
